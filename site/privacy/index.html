Privacy

This page covers this landing site. Each app's own notes are at the end.

The short version

  • No cookies. No third-party scripts, fonts, pixels or embeds — everything is served from this site.
  • One tiny, first-party count per page view and per button click, and nothing at all if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • No IP addresses and no user-agent strings are stored. Nothing is sold or shared.

What a count contains

When you open a page or click an “open app” / “download” button, your browser sends this, and only this:

FieldExampleWhy
typepageview or clickto count visits and button clicks
path/busboardwhich page
ref_hostwww.google.comthe site that linked here (host name only, never the full address)
targetbusboard:apkwhich button, for clicks only

On receipt the server adds: the time; your country as reported by the hosting provider (two letters, e.g. IN); a device class (mobile, tablet or desktop) and a likely-bot flag, both derived from the user-agent string, which is then thrown away; and a visitor hash used only to count unique visitors per day.

The visitor hash

It is computed from your IP address and browser string with a random secret that changes every day; the old secrets are deleted. It therefore cannot be reversed to an IP address, and the same person produces unrelated hashes on different days — no profile across days is possible. The same daily-salted hash is used for rate-limiting abuse.

Opting out

Turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser (or use an extension that does), and this site sends nothing. Blocking scripts works too: the pages read fine without them.

Storage on your device

The Bus Board page remembers whether you chose its “day” skin in your browser's local storage. That value never leaves your device. Nothing else is stored.

Retention

Counts are kept for about 13 months to see trends, then deleted.

The apps themselves

Harahachi — accounts are a username and PIN; each phone is paired with a single-use QR token and gets its own key; your meals and profile belong to your account; AI analysis runs on the server and the AI backends are never exposed to the phone.

Bus Board — sharing your ride is optional. The first and last 200 m of every shared trace are discarded before storage; your contributor id is a random id held by the app that you can discard; the app has no analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDK.
