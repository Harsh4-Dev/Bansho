HARAHACHI

Eat until you are eight-tenths full.

A photo-first calorie & macro tracker, styled as a journey across an old Japanese map. Snap your dish, a vision model itemizes it around the food, and one hanko stamp logs it.

  • Your daily goal is a day's walk along the Tōkaidō road
  • Camera analysis, barcode scanning, one-tap quick-log
  • Annai, the chat guide: “I ate 2 idlis and sambar” logs it
