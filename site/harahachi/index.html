道Your day is a road

Your daily calorie goal is a day's walk along the Tōkaidō road on a dense ukiyo-e map — ink mountains, pine forests, villages, temples, a deep indigo sea. Every meal moves your traveler forward. Workouts extend the road. Landing at the destination station means you ate hara hachi bu — just enough.

An example day: 1,430 / 2,200 kcal · 里 6.5 of 10

There is no tab bar. You tap a hanko seal to zoom into a region, swipe down to close it, and swipe the header to travel to the next station. Every panel is glass with a frost you can tune yourself.

茶What it does

Photo-first, but never only a photo.

  • Camera analysis that surrounds the foodOne glass chip per component — dal · rice · roti ×2 · curd — pinned beside its spot with a leader line, portions cycling ×0.5–×2, and one clarifying question when it matters (“ghee on the rotis?”).
  • Barcode scanningPackaged food via OpenFoodFacts, proxied and cached by the server.
  • The feedback loopWeight trend, goal pace turned into a calorie target (Katch-McArdle with body fat), an arrival date, streaks with red temple stamps and gold milestones, weekly insights in the guide's voice.
  • RemindersA 6 pm goal nudge suggesting your regular meals, 3 pm water, a 10 pm recap.
  • Offline-firstLogs queue on the phone and sync when the server returns; every failure is a popup with retry, never a stuck screen.
  • AccountsUsername + PIN, separate data per user, logout and switch.

関Guarded like a checkpoint

Devices get their own keys from single-use QR pairing tokens (stored hashed). PIN accounts lock after repeated failures, every device is rate-limited, admin rights are enforced by the server, and the AI backends are never exposed to the phone.
