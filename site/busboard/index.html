[/>why headway, not timetables

TNSTC publishes no realtime feed, no GTFS and no itemised departure times for the Trichy->Thanjavur direction. It is a 24-hour corridor running every 10-15 minutes, so the board says "next bus ~4 min, then ~11, ~19" rather than naming services it cannot know.

[/>three tiers, never collapsed

@ live
a contributor is aboard and the sighting is fresh
* recent
last ping 1-15 min old - dead-reckoned, with its uncertainty shown
o sched
nobody has seen this bus - from the schedule alone

The word and the glyph carry the meaning, not colour, so the board reads the same to anyone. It never presents a guess as an observation.

White on black because a rider is reading one number at arm's length in Tamil Nadu sun. The map is a text plot of the route's own geometry: no tiles, no keys, no WebGL.

[/>share your ride

  • Riders can share location while aboard; it is what turns a scheduled bus into a live one.
  • Every trace passes validation gates biased toward rejection - a bench, a walk or a car does not look like a bus.
  • Trust is earned slowly and lost five times faster. A solo ride raises nobody's standing.
  • It works on day one with zero contributors, and gets better, never worse, as contributions arrive.

[/>privacy

  • The first and last 200 m of every shared trace are discarded before storage - before any other code sees it.
  • Your contributor id is a random id kept in the app's own storage, and can be discarded. Ride session ids rotate per ride.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK.

[/>also on the board

  • SASTRA's own campus buses, as a separate layer from their published stop-level timetable (the board says how old that timetable is).
  • A real command line: to thanjavur, share, stops, refresh.
  • An Android app that keeps reporting with the screen off while you share a ride.

[/>honest status

The corridor seed is provisional: stop positions are still unverified and the route shape is approximate. The data says so itself, and it improves as stops are surveyed.
